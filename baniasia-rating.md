Project Structure Rating: 4 / 5

The folders are organized well. Pages, layouts, models, and services each have their own place, and the names follow normal rules, so things are easy to find. The commits are a strong point, since they use clear tags like feat:, style:, and refactor:, which makes the history easy to read. A few things bring the score down: the first commit added the whole app at once instead of building it up step by step, and some auto-generated Tailwind files got committed by mistake.

Front-End Rating: 4 / 5

The site looks polished. The pink and berry colors are used the same way on every page, and the fonts give it a nice bakery feel. It's easy to scroll through, from the top banner down to the products and reviews, and each section is separated clearly. The product cards look neat and consistent, and the footer is tidy. The only downside is that a few placeholder images don't quite match the pastry names.
